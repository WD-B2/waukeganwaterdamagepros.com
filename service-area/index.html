Waukegan and Lake County

Water Damage Restoration Service Area Around Waukegan, IL

Dispatch is based in Waukegan, the Lake County seat, and covers nearby towns day and night. Here is how the area fits together.

Waukegan sits on the Lake Michigan shoreline in Lake County, Illinois. The city uses ZIP codes 60085, 60087 and 60079, and dispatch covers all three. From Waukegan, crews reach the villages to the west and northwest in a short drive, though traffic and weather change travel time.

Waukegan neighborhoods and housing

Waukegan has a wide mix of housing. The Near North Historic District holds buildings dating from the 1840s to 1928, and the district has its own page. Newer subdivisions can have finished basements with hidden supply lines. Either way, the crew reads the structure before choosing equipment.

The city water system draws from Lake Michigan through two raw water intakes. For flood risk at your own address, use the FEMA Flood Map Service Center, and ask the City of Waukegan about local floodplain rules. We do not name flood zones for single streets.

Nearby towns

Two nearby villages have their own pages with local notes. Wadsworth is about six miles northwest, and Lake Villa is about seven miles west. If your town is not listed, call and ask. Dispatch can tell you whether a crew can reach you.

The three ZIP codes shown above are the ones tied to the city itself. Addresses near a boundary can carry a neighboring ZIP code even when the street is in Waukegan, so give dispatch the street address and town as well as the ZIP code.

What to tell dispatch

  • Your street address and town
  • The source of the water, if you know it
  • How deep the water is and which rooms it has reached
  • Whether the main water valve is closed
  • Whether anyone is on a private well or septic system

That information lets the dispatcher plan equipment and explain what happens first. It also helps to say if sewage may be involved, because that changes the protective steps. See sewage backup cleanup for what that looks like.

How travel and weather affect response

Lake County roads are busy at commuting hours, and lake-effect weather can slow a drive in winter. Dispatch accounts for this when it books a crew, and it will tell you honestly if a crew is stretched. A busy week with many storm calls means a longer wait, even when same-day visits are offered when a crew is available.

If you can reduce risk while you wait, do it. Close the main, lift valuables and keep people out of the wet area. These steps cost nothing and they limit the damage a crew has to deal with on arrival.

Seasons that keep dispatch busy

Calls follow the weather. Hard freezes bring cracked pipes, spring thaws and heavy rain bring wet basements, and summer humidity brings air conditioner drain overflows. Knowing the pattern helps you check pipes in November, test the sump pump in March and clear drains before storms.

A call in a busy week may take longer to schedule, which is one more reason to describe the water clearly and call early.

Businesses, rentals and second homes

Property managers and owners who live elsewhere can call on behalf of a tenant or a tenant business. Give dispatch the property address and an on-site contact. The dispatcher will explain what access the crew needs and what to tell the occupant.

Getting ready before a crew arrives

Clear a path from the door to the wet area and move small items out of the way. Unlock gates, and secure pets in another room. If the driveway is icy or the street is narrow, tell dispatch so the crew can plan where to park and how to carry equipment.

Know where the main water valve, the electrical panel and any sump pump are. Crews ask about all three, and having answers ready shortens the first conversation.

Services across the area

Every service on the services page is available throughout the service area. The most common calls are basement water damage, burst pipes and water extraction. Commercial buildings can use commercial water damage restoration.

Waukegan neighborhoods with their own pages

  • Near North Historic District: a historic residential area, with notes on caring for older floors, plaster and trim.
  • Downtown Waukegan: shops, offices and apartments where water can cross shared walls.

Towns with their own pages

  • Wadsworth, IL (ZIP 60083): river side of Lake County, with notes on clay soil, wells and village permits.
  • Lake Villa, IL (ZIP 60046): western Lake County, with notes on the village water and sewer system.
