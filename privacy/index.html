Privacy Policy

This page explains what information we handle when you use this website or call us.

(847) 368-7353

Waukegan Water Damage Pros operates this website. We aim to collect as little personal information as we can. This site has no forms and no account system.

Information you give us

When you call, we collect what you choose to tell us, such as your name, address and a description of the damage. We use it to arrange service and to follow up on the job. When you email us, we keep the message and your address so we can reply.

Third-party content

Pages on this site load fonts from Google Fonts and maps from Google Maps. Those services may receive technical data such as your IP address when the content loads. Their handling of that data is covered by their own policies.

How we use information

We use information to provide service, to respond to questions and to meet legal and insurance obligations. We do not sell personal information.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete information you gave us by emailing contact@waukeganwaterdamagepros.com. We may need to keep some job records where the law or an insurance process requires it.

Changes

We may update this page. The version on this page is the current one.
