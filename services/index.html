Water Damage Restoration Services in Waukegan, IL

Pick the service that matches your situation, or call dispatch and describe it. Each page explains the steps, the safety points and what affects scope and price.

Water damage comes from many sources, and the right response depends on the source. Clean supply water, rain water and sewage are handled differently, and so are a wet carpet, a soaked wall cavity and a flooded crawl space. The services below are grouped by where the water came from or what it touched.

If you are not sure which one fits, call. The dispatcher asks a few plain questions and tells you what will happen first. An available crew is sent the same day, and the scope and price are explained before work starts.

Choosing the right service

How to choose

Start with the source. A split pipe belongs with plumbing failures, a leaking tank with appliances, a rain-driven flood with weather. If water is already on the floor, water extraction is the first step in almost every case.

Water types and why they matter

Restoration professionals sort water into categories by how clean it is. Clean supply water from a pipe or tank is the simplest case. Water that has touched flooring, soil or a drain carries more contamination, and sewage is the most serious. The category decides which materials can be dried and which must be removed.

Time changes the category too. Clean water that sits for a day or two can become contaminated as bacteria grow. That is why a quick call matters even when the source looks harmless.

Three things every job includes

Whatever the service, the process follows the same order. First the source is stopped or located. Then standing and trapped water is removed. Then the structure is dried and moisture readings are taken, so you know the materials are dry before any rebuilding starts.

Mold and sewage add steps. Mold remediation adds containment and independent clearance testing. Sewage backup cleanup adds protective gear and removal of porous materials that touched the water.

All 20 water damage services

Pipes and plumbing

  • Burst Pipe Water Damage: Shutoff guidance, water removal and drying after a pipe splits.
  • Frozen Pipe Water Damage: Cleanup after a frozen line cracks and releases water on thawing.
  • Water Heater Leak Cleanup: Water removal after a tank leaks or fails.
  • Slab Leak Water Damage: Moisture control when a pipe under the slab leaks.

Appliances and HVAC

  • Appliance Leak Water Damage: Cleanup after a washer, dishwasher or refrigerator line leaks.
  • AC Leak Water Damage: Drying after a clogged condensate line or air handler overflow.
  • Kitchen Water Damage: Cabinet, floor and wall drying after kitchen leaks.

Weather and building envelope

  • Ceiling Water Damage Repair: Dry-out and repair of wet, stained or sagging ceilings.
  • Storm Water Damage: Cleanup after rain or storm water gets into a building.
  • Flood Damage Cleanup: Cleanup and drying after outside water enters the building.
  • Basement Water Damage: Pump-out, drying and cleanup of flooded lower levels.
  • Crawl Space Water Damage: Water removal and drying in crawl spaces.

Water removal and drying

  • Water Extraction: Pumps and extraction tools remove standing water before it reaches the subfloor.
  • Structural Drying: Controlled drying of walls, floors and framing with moisture readings.
  • Sewage Backup Cleanup: Protective-gear removal and sanitizing after a drain backs up.

Floors and walls

  • Hardwood Floor Water Damage: Floor drying and repair when hardwood cups or buckles.
  • Carpet Water Damage: Carpet and pad drying, cleaning or replacement.
  • Wall and Drywall Water Damage: Drying and repair of wet walls, insulation and drywall.

Commercial and mold

  • Commercial Water Damage: Larger drying plans for stores, offices and apartment buildings.
  • Mold Remediation: Containment, removal and clearance testing for mold growth.
