Emergency Frozen Pipe Water Damage in Waukegan, IL

Water damage from a frozen pipe usually shows up at the thaw. Shut the water off, call dispatch, and we handle the cleanup.

(847) 368-7353

Frozen pipe water damage in Waukegan is the flooding that follows when ice splits a supply line and the thaw releases the water. The pipe often breaks while frozen and only leaks once it warms, so the damage can start hours after you thought the worst had passed. If you see water, call the 24/7 dispatch line and close the main valve first.

Frozen pipe emergency: act at the first sign of water

A frozen pipe that has not yet split is a prevention problem. A frozen pipe that is leaking is an emergency. If a faucet runs slowly or not at all in cold weather, the line may be frozen in the wall, and the first leak can appear when heat returns to that wall.

  • Close the main shutoff as soon as you see water or a wet spot where a pipe runs.
  • Do not use a torch or open flame to thaw a pipe, which has started house fires. A hair dryer on low heat, applied only to a pipe that is not leaking, is the safer method.
  • Keep the heat on and open cabinet doors under sinks on exterior walls.
  • If water has reached electrical outlets or fixtures, stay clear and call the utility or 911 before you call us.

How a freeze turns into a flood

Water expands as it turns to ice, and the pressure builds in the pipe between the ice and the closed faucet, not under the ice itself. That is why the split often appears in a section that looks fine from the outside. Pipes in exterior walls, unheated garages, attics and crawl spaces freeze first because they sit outside the heated envelope.

Northern Illinois winters bring repeated freezing and thawing, and each thaw is a chance for a hidden split to start leaking. For actual freeze statistics for your address, check the National Weather Service Chicago office. This page works from how pipes fail, not from a local frequency we cannot source.

What the cleanup looks like

Crews find the wet area with a moisture meter and thermal camera, because water from a split pipe often tracks along framing. Standing water goes first, then soft materials are removed where drying in place will not work. Insulation that has absorbed water usually has to come out of the cavity.

Air movers and dehumidifiers follow, running until moisture readings fall back to dry. Walls that were opened for drying can then be patched. If water ran down into a lower level, water extraction and basement water damage cleanup describe the first steps there.

Frozen pipes in Waukegan homes

In any older house, a line that runs along an exterior wall or an unheated porch is a common weak spot in freezing weather. When you hire a plumber for the repair, ask where else in the house a pipe runs along a cold surface.

Homes near the lakefront and the harbor can feel colder when wind comes off Lake Michigan, though we do not have a measured figure to quote for that. The practical point is simple. Know where your main shutoff is before winter, because the first minute decides how much water gets out.

Frequently asked questions

Do you handle frozen pipe emergencies at night or on weekends in Waukegan?

Yes. Dispatch answers around the clock every day, including during cold snaps when calls are heavy. Close the main valve and tell the dispatcher where the pipe runs.

Why did my pipe leak hours after the weather warmed up?

Ice can plug a split pipe. When it melts, water flows through the crack. The break happened during the freeze, but the flooding began at the thaw.

Is it safe to thaw a frozen pipe myself?

Only a pipe that is not leaking, and only with gentle heat such as a hair dryer on low. Never use an open flame. If you are unsure where the frozen section is, call a plumber.

Can a frozen pipe cause mold?

Wet insulation and drywall that stay damp can support mold growth. Drying quickly after the leak is the best prevention, and mold remediation is a separate service if growth has already started.

Does insurance cover freeze damage?

Many homeowners policies cover sudden freeze damage, sometimes with conditions about heat or vacancy. Check your wording or ask your insurer, and keep photos and the repair invoice.
